# Academics: a guide for coordinators and teachers

## Who does what

| Person               | Does                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Academic coordinator | Classes and sections, subjects, teacher assignments, timetable, substitutions, lesson-plan approvals, promotion |
| Class teacher        | Daily work for the section, attendance, notices to the section, the exam register                               |
| Subject teacher      | Homework, classwork and assignments for their subject; marks                                                    |
| Office               | Notices and circulars, the almanac and holidays, gallery, document templates, transfer certificates             |

## Setup (once a year)

1. **Academics → Classes and sections** (roll numbers per section), **Subjects** with display order.
2. **Teacher assignments**: class teacher and subject teachers per section; the assignment grants the
   scoped role automatically and ends it when the assignment ends.
3. **Timetable**: periods and slots; a teacher cannot be in two sections in the same period.
4. **Holidays and almanac**: the school calendar the apps show.

## Daily routine

- Teachers post **Daily work** (homework, classwork, assignments) from the teacher app; families see it
  at once. Attachments are scanned before they are visible.
- **Substitutions** for absent teachers; families of the section see the change.
- **Notices**: class, wing or school-wide; staff-only circulars stay in the teacher app.
- **Lesson plans**: weekly plans with the three-level approval (coordinator → head → principal).

## Year end

- **People → Promotion**: decisions per pupil (promote, detain, leave), applied into the new year's
  enrolments; sections of the new year are created from the current structure.
- **Transfer certificates** and withdrawals from **People**.

## Five common questions

1. _A teacher cannot post for a section._ Add the teacher assignment for that section this year.
2. _The timetable refuses a slot._ Another section already has that teacher in the period; check the
   conflict message and move one of them.
3. _A notice must reach only one class._ Target the class or section when creating it.
4. _Homework posted by mistake._ Delete it the same day from the teacher app; families see the update.
5. _Who approved the lesson plan?_ The plan's history lists each level with the name and time.
