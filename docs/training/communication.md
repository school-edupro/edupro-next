# Communication: a guide for the office and the principal

## Who does what

| Person        | Does                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Office        | Drafts message requests (SMS, WhatsApp, email, push) to groups; maintains templates; records signed consents |
| Principal     | Approves every general message before dispatch                                                               |
| Class teacher | Notices to the section; answers family queries of the section                                                |
| Families      | Choose what they receive (consents); read notices; raise queries                                             |

## Sending a message

1. **Communication → Templates**: SMS templates carry their DLT id; WhatsApp templates their approved
   name; push templates are for the apps only.
2. **Communication → Requests → New**: template, variables, recipients (class, section, group, list);
   **Preview** shows how many will receive it, how many are skipped and why (no address, consent
   withdrawn, duplicate).
3. Submit; the principal approves in the inbox; dispatch happens on approval. Requesters can cancel
   until then.
4. **Outbox** shows each message with its delivery status from the provider's receipts.

## Consents and privacy

- General messages respect each family's consent per channel; service messages (fees, attendance,
  safety) always go.
- Families change their consents in the parent app; the office records signed forms under
  **Communication → Consent** with a note.
- The privacy notice is versioned; families acknowledge a new version once in the app.

## Five common questions

1. _Why were 12 recipients skipped?_ Open the request: each skip is listed with its reason.
2. _The message shows "sent" but the parent did not get it._ Check the delivery status; "delivered"
   needs the provider's receipt; check the number on the profile.
3. _Can a teacher send a WhatsApp to the class?_ Teachers post notices; messages go through a request
   and the principal's approval.
4. _A family wants no marketing but fee reminders._ Withdraw the marketing purpose; fee reminders are
   service messages.
5. _Where is the record that a parent agreed?_ The consent history per person under the office view,
   with source, version, who recorded it and when.
