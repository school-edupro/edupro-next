# Employee daily activity log

Every employee writes what they did in the day. Submitting is enough; the head reviews by exception.

## My day (every employee)
- Teacher app → **My day (activity log)**; admin portal → People → **Daily activity log**.
- The day is a table of time slots: **From, To, Category, What was done**.
- An empty day starts with the periods of your timetable and your substitutions for that date. Correct them, empty a row that did not happen, and add the rest (meetings, duty, correction, office work).
- Optional boxes: *Pending / carried forward* and *Plan for tomorrow*.
- **Save draft** keeps it; **Submit my day** sends it. Two slots may not overlap.
- Submitted after the cut-off time, or on a later day, is marked **late**.
- After submitting, the day is locked unless it is sent back to you. A day can be filled up to the number of days the school allows.
- The last seven days show on top with their state.

## Review (principal, coordinator, admin)
- **Review** lists every active employee for a date: submitted, not filled, sent back, late. The tiles filter the list; department filter; Excel and PDF of the day.
- Open a log to read it. *Mark reviewed* (remark optional) or *Send back* (remark needed); a log sent back opens again for the employee.

## Dashboard and reports
- Tiles: share of working days submitted, today's count, late and sent back, hours logged.
- Charts: logs per day (and late), submission by department, where the time goes (hours by category), who has not submitted today.
- **Submission by employee**: working days, submitted, not filled, late, %, time logged — Excel and PDF; per employee a day-by-day sheet.
- Working days leave out Sundays and holidays that apply to employees.

## Set-up (admin)
- Submit-by time, and how many days later a day may be filled.
- Categories of work: rename, reorder, mark *Not used*, add new.

Not included yet: an evening reminder message, attachments on a row, and an employee-leave link (an employee on leave shows as "not filled").
