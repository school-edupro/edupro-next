# Exams and report cards: a guide for the examination cell and teachers

## Who does what

| Person               | Does                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Academic coordinator | Exam types and grade scales, exams and their subjects, entry locks, report-card templates and releases |
| Subject teacher      | Enters marks for their subject and sections (teacher app or admin)                                     |
| Class teacher        | Indicators, remarks, exam attendance, height and weight (the exam register)                            |
| Principal            | Reads results analytics; approves the release                                                          |
| Families             | Read the released card in the parent app and download the PDF (unless withheld for fee default)        |

## Before the exam

1. **Exams → Masters**: exam types (PT, half-yearly, annual) and the grade scale per class band.
2. **Exams → Exams → New**: name, dates, the classes and subjects with maximum and pass marks; tick
   **show on portal** so the datesheet appears in the apps.
3. Optional: indicator sets (primary), the remark bank.

## Marks entry

- Teachers enter marks in the teacher app **Marks** (or **Exams → open exam → Entry**): a row per
  pupil, absent and exempt switches, saved as they go; the range is checked against the maximum.
- The coordinator **locks** a subject when entry is complete; a reopen needs a reason and is audited.
- The exam register (indicators, remarks, exam attendance, health) belongs to the class teacher.

## Results and report cards

1. **Exams → open exam → Results → Compute**: totals, percentages, grades, ranks per section and class.
2. **Exams → Report cards → Templates**: the layout per band (HPC for primary, CCE for middle,
   components for secondary, theory/practical for senior); preview with a real pupil.
3. **Exams → Report cards → New release**: choose the term, the exams and the template per band; tick
   **hide defaulters** with the minimum balance if the school withholds cards; release.
4. Generate the batch PDF per section from the release; families download their own from the app.
5. **Insights → Results analytics**: pass % and mean by class and section, weakest subjects.

## Five common questions

1. _A teacher cannot see a section._ They need a teacher assignment for that section and subject this
   year (**Academics → Teacher assignments**).
2. _The card shows the wrong grade._ Check the grade scale assigned to the class for this exam;
   recompute results after fixing it.
3. _A family cannot download the card._ Either the release is not yet released, or the card is
   withheld for a fee balance above the release's minimum.
4. _We need to change a mark after locking._ Reopen the subject (coordinator), correct, lock again;
   recompute results and re-render the card.
5. _Board results._ **Exams → Board results**: upload the CBSE file; pupils match by board roll number
   (kept on the enrolment) or by name; the analysis and the export are available at once.

## Teaching subjects and marks-entry subjects (October 2026)

- **Subject groups.** Academics → Subjects: a subject can be _part of_ another. Physics, Chemistry and
  Biology are part of Science. Teachers are mapped to the parts, the timetable and daily work use the
  parts, and the report card shows Science. One level only.
- **Daily work.** A subject teacher gives homework and classwork only for the subjects mapped to them
  in that class; the class teacher and coordinators for any.
- **Exam parts.** Exams → open the exam → _Subjects entered in parts_: leave a subject as one figure,
  or split it into parts with their own maximum: Theory and Practical, or one part per teaching
  subject (the page suggests them). The subject's maximum becomes the sum of the parts.
- **Entering.** In the teacher app, Marks lists each part the teacher may enter (Science · Physics);
  each teacher enters their own part, the class teacher and coordinators any. The subject's marks are
  the sum of the parts; a pupil absent in every part is absent in the subject. Registers, analysis,
  report cards and promotion read that total.
- **Rules.** A subject already marked as one figure cannot be split until those marks are cleared; a
  part with marks cannot be removed, and its maximum cannot go below the marks entered.
