# Admissions: a guide for the office

Online admission works per admission session (one per academic year it admits into). Families register
on the school's site, `/<school>/admission`; the office works in the **Admissions** menu.

## Who does what

| Person                      | Does                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Admissions in-charge        | Forms, sessions, fees, set-up (stages and processes), selection, Admit                 |
| Front office / receptionist | Enquiries and follow-ups, walk-in registrations at the desk                            |
| Approvers                   | Decide at their level: Document check, test or interview marks, interaction, selection |
| Accounts                    | Registration and admission fees at the counter; approve and pay admission fee refunds  |
| Families                    | Register online, pay, correct when asked, download receipts, forms and the admit card  |

## Setting up a session (once a year)

1. **Registration forms**: start from a ready form (Class IX general, Class XI streams) or a blank one; edit
   tabs and questions; mark which answers go to the student master.
2. **Admission set-up**: how families get their sign-in code (email, SMS, WhatsApp), the receipt series
   (RF and AF), the fee categories (EWS, staff ward, sibling … decided from the form's answers), the stages
   and the **approval processes** (for example Primary: Document check, Interaction, Selection; Senior:
   Document check, Written test, Interview, Selection) with the approvers of each level, given to classes.
3. **Admission sessions → the session**: classes with their form, seats, dates and age rule; the fee table
   (registration and admission fee per class and category, seats per category); offers' days and the
   refund deduction; the public link, QR and poster.

## The flow

1. **Enquiries**: note each enquiry (walk-in, call, website), follow up on the date set, and **invite** the
   family: the link starts their form with the child's details.
2. **Registration**: the family signs in with a code, fills the form tab by tab, submits and pays the
   registration fee online; the registration number, receipt and form come by email. At the
   **Admission desk** the office fills the form for a walk-in parent and takes the fee in cash or UPI.
3. **Registrations**: open one to decide at your level. Document check confirms the category; **Send back**
   marks what to correct (the family is emailed and changes only those answers). **Undo** puts a decision
   back.
4. **Tests and interviews**: add slots, allot the waiting candidates (they get the schedule and an admit
   card), then record attendance, marks and pass / not selected for the whole slot.
5. **Selection**: offer from the merit list or by draw within the seats of each category; the rest wait in
   order. An offer not paid by its date lapses; **Offer the next** fills the seat.
6. **Admission fee**: paid in the portal or at the counter from the registration page; the admission number
   is given then, with the AF receipt and the admission form.
7. **Admit**: give each candidate a class-section (one by one, or **Admit** for a whole class with "Spread
   over the sections"); the pupil, parents, documents and photos go to the student master and the parents
   are told to use the parent app.
8. **Cancel** from the registration page: before the admission fee nothing is refunded; after it a refund
   less the session's deduction waits in **Admission fee refunds** for the refund approvers.

## Reports

**Dashboard** (figures, the funnel per class), **Admissions list** (any column, filter and export, saved
view), **Admission fee collection** (fees and refunds by date, class and mode; also in Fees → Day book under
the admission ledger), **Enquiries → Conversion report**, and the **Report builder** (dataset Admissions).

## Five common questions

1. _The family did not get the code._ The page says where it went; email can take a minute (spam folder),
   and they may sign in with the registration number once they have one.
2. _They paid but the registration number has not come._ Check the payment under Payments; a late gateway
   reply registers them when it arrives. At the desk, take the fee only if the online payment failed.
3. _A wrong answer after submitting._ Correct it yourself under **Corrections by the office** on the
   registration page (the old and new answers and your reason are kept), or Send back the field so the
   family corrects it and it returns to the same level. Files and photos always go back to the family.
   After Admit, use **Correct details** on the student page (with approval).
4. _A class has no process._ Its registrations wait; give the class a process in Admission set-up (changes
   that reach registrations in progress ask for confirmation).
5. _Admit says the class has no section._ Create the sections of the session's year under Academics first.

## After Admit: correcting a mistake

- **Wrong name, date of birth, gender or parent's name**: student page → Profile & guardians →
  **Correct details**. The principal (or the levels set under Approval set-up) approves; old and new
  values are kept.
- **Wrong class**: student page → Academics → **Correct class**. Choose the right class-section and,
  if not from the admission date, the date it applies from; **See what changes** shows the new roll
  number and the fee before and after. Once approved, the pupil moves, the fee is made again at the new
  class's rates and money already paid is set against it; anything left over stays as advance on the
  receipt and is given back on request (Refunds).
