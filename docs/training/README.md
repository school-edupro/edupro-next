# Training material (Release 1)

One page per module, written for the people who will use it every day. Each page follows the same
shape: who does what, the daily routine, the periodic routine, the five most common questions, and
where to click. The in-app tours (the **Show me around** button in the admin header) walk the same
screens live; the demo walk-throughs in `docs/demo-logins.md` give the hands-on exercises used in the
training sessions.

| Module        | Page                                 | Sessions                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Admissions    | [admissions.md](admissions.md)       | Office (1 session, 90 min)                   |
| Academics     | [academics.md](academics.md)         | Coordinators (1), teachers per wing (1 each) |
| Attendance    | [attendance.md](attendance.md)       | Teachers with academics; office (30 min)     |
| Communication | [communication.md](communication.md) | Office and principal (60 min)                |
| Engagement    | [engagement.md](engagement.md)       | Office, class teachers (60 min)              |
| Fees          | [fees.md](fees.md)                   | Accounts (2 sessions, 90 min each)           |
| Exams         | [exams.md](exams.md)                 | Examination cell, teachers (90 min)          |

Session format: 20 minutes of demonstration on the demo school, 40 minutes of hands-on with the
exercises, 20 minutes of questions; the trainer records questions that need a change in the product in
the backlog. Every trainee signs in with their own account during the session and completes the
in-app tour of their home screen.
