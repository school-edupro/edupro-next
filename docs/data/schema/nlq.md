## nlq.academic_years

One row is one academic year of the school (school session, e.g. 2026-27, usually April to March). Every year is here: planned (next year being set up), active (the working year), locked and closed (past years). For "this year" filter is_current = true; for "last year" take the year whose end_date is just before the current one's start_date. Other nlq views carry academic_year_id and academic_year (the same code), so join on academic_year_id or filter on academic_year directly. Admission sessions point to the year the children will join, usually the next one.

Primary key: — · row-level security: off

| academic_year | text | The year's short code as the school writes it, e.g. 2026-27. The same text appears as academic_year in other nlq views. |
| name | text | The year's display name, e.g. Session 2026-27. |
| start_date | date | First day of the academic year. |
| end_date | date | Last day of the academic year. |
| status | text | planned (not started, being set up), active (the working year), locked (past year, closed for most changes) or closed (archived past year). |
| is_current | boolean | True for exactly one year: the school's working year (status active; the latest-starting one if two are active). False for all others. |

## nlq.admission_applications

One row is one child's admission application in one admission session (called a registration once it has a registration number), from form submitted to admitted, rejected or cancelled. Left out: drafts (forms started but not submitted) and applications of removed sessions; no names or contact details of the child or family. Admitted means is_admitted = true (status fee_paid or admitted), as the school's admission reports count; in_student_master = the student record is made; ever_admitted also keeps those admitted and cancelled later. Registered = is_registered (registration fee paid or none due). Filter one admission_session (or academic_year, the year the children join, usually next year), group by class (sort class_order), status_label, fee_category or stage. Dates are India dates: group registrations by registered_on, offers by offered_on, admissions by fee_paid_on. Money is rupees: *_paid are receipts actually taken (reversed and bounced left out), refunded = refunds paid out in money, moved_out = money carried to another school of the group. A registration moved to another school shows here as cancelled. Join nlq.admission_enquiries on application_id; nlq.students on student_id for the class-section after admission.

## nlq.admission_enquiries

One row is one admission enquiry (a lead): a family asking about admission by walk-in, call, the website or a campaign, with its source, class asked for, stage on the enquiry board, counsellor, follow-up dates, and whether the family registered and was admitted. Every enquiry is here, open or closed; no personal details of the family or child are. Funnel: enquiries -> stage_kind started (registration form begun) -> is_registered (registration completed, registration number given; status converted) -> is_admitted (admission fee paid or admitted, as the school's enquiry report counts). Lost = is_lost (not interested or closed). Filter by enquired_on (or month) for enquiries taken in a period, or by admission_session / academic_year (the year the child would join). Group by source, class (sort class_order), counsellor, stage or score. For the registration itself join nlq.admission_applications on application_id.

## nlq.assets

One row is one unit of an asset item in the asset register (one tag: a laptop, a projector, a lab microscope). All assets are listed, also those that have left the books; filter is_on_books for the register as it stands (in store, in use, in transit, under repair, lost). Money in rupees. book_value is the closing value of the latest financial year for which depreciation was run (book_value_year), else the cost: it does not fall between yearly runs. Use: count or sum cost / book_value by category, department, store or status; who holds what = employee_name (the custodian). An asset item kept in a store is also counted in nlq.stock_balances, so do not add asset values to stock values. Join nlq.staff on employee_id for the custodian department or whether they still work here.

## nlq.attendance_days

One row is one pupil's mark in the class's day register on one school day (the daily attendance the class teacher takes; subject-period attendance is not here). Only days with a register appear: holidays and weekly offs have no rows, and a pupil the teacher did not mark has no row that day (not marked is not absent). Left out: removed, merged or erased student records, pupils whose admission that year was cancelled, and marks of old closed years the school has archived. Counting rule (the register's own): days = rows; present = is_present (every code except A absent and LV leave; late, half day, short leave, on duty and stay back count as present, a half day as a whole day); attendance % = 100 * count(*) FILTER (WHERE is_present) / count(*), so leave lowers the %. For % without leave days use count(*) FILTER (WHERE NOT is_leave) as the base, and say so. Filter on on_date (a date range is fastest; month is for grouping) and academic_year; group by class_section, class (sort class_order) or student. class / section are those of the register the mark is in (the pupil's section that day). Join nlq.students on student_id and academic_year_id for gender, category or house.

## nlq.certificates

One row is one certificate paper in the certificate register: an original (copy_no 0) or a duplicate of it (same number, copy_no 1, 2 ...), for a student or a staff member: transfer certificate (type_code TC), bonafide, character, date of birth, fee paid, award certificates, experience and others. Every TC is here (use this view, not any other TC list). Cancelled papers are kept (status cancelled). Use: certificates issued = count rows WHERE copy_no = 0 (originals); valid now = is_valid; duplicates = is_duplicate. Group by type, month (of issued_on) or academic_year. academic_year is the year recorded on the certificate (the year the office was working in), which can differ from the year of issued_on around April. The class is the student enrolment in that academic year. A re-issue cancels the old paper and gives a new number (is_reissue, reissued_as_number); a correction keeping the number raises revision and moves issued_on to the correction day (first_issued_on keeps the first). is_old_system rows came from the previous software. The printed details of the paper are not available here.

## nlq.class_sections

One row is one class-section (e.g. VI-A) of one academic year, with its class teacher and number of pupils. Sections are per year: VI-A of 2025-26 and VI-A of 2026-27 are two rows, so filter is_current_year = true (or one academic_year) first. Removed sections are left out. strength counts pupils the way the school's strength reports do: for the current or a future year, pupils whose enrolment is active now; for a year already over, pupils on the section's roll on the year's last day. Cancelled admissions and removed or merged student records never count. Sum strength by class for class strength (sort by class_order). For pupil-level detail use nlq.students on class_section_id.

## nlq.exam_marks

One row is one pupil's paper (subject) in one exam: pupil × exam × subject, for every paper the pupil takes, whether marks are entered yet or not (is_entered). A paper the pupil does not take (subject choices, mid-year subject changes) has no row. Left out: deleted exams, deleted, merged or erased pupils, cancelled enrolments; a pupil who left the school, and an exam set up without a marks area (indicators or remarks only), keep only the papers that have an entry. Marks are the raw entries of the marks sheets: they include sheets still open or only submitted — for official (final) figures keep is_verified = true. ## nlq.fee_bills

One row is one bill line of a pupil: one fee head (Tuition, Transport, Previous dues, Bounce charge ...) for one fee month of one academic year, on the school ledger or the hostel ledger. This is what the school asks the family to pay and what has been paid against it. Left out: lines taken off the bill (cancelled: months after a pupil left, hostel months not stayed), old-year lines carried into the next year's opening balance (carried; the same money is on the new year's Previous dues lines), removed and merged duplicate pupils. The LATE FEE IS NOT IN THIS VIEW: it is worked out live from the day of payment and is not a bill line until collected (collected late fee is in nlq.fee_receipts.late_fee); only last year's unpaid late fine, carried forward, appears here as head_kind late_fee.
