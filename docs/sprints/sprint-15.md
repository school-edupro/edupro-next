## 3. Decisions

- **The dataset registry is the report framework** (ADR-013); Kysely and materialised views are not
  introduced. The plan's wording is superseded and recorded here.
- **Live tables for the day book and tally, marts for the year-wide views**: a day book must agree with the
  counter the same minute; forecast and defaulters may lag the 15-minute refresh.
- **Scoped catalogue entries instead of rewriting the staff catalogue**: the ids come from the viewer, never
  from the model; a scoped caller cannot reach an unscoped entry, so no leak is possible by prompt.
- **Families opt in** to the assistant (`ai.assistant` consent must be granted); staff do not (the purpose
  is the school's operation, and the audit trail applies to both).
- **Returned cheques are flagged, not posted**: the bounce stays an approval with its charge (Sprint 14).
- **Health values stay out of the audit payload**; the row trigger keeps the change with key masking.

## 5. What the tests found before release

- **`ON CONFLICT … RETURNING (xmax = 0)`** is the only reliable way to count inserted versus updated rows in
  `app.enter_marks`; re-reading `xmax` after the statement counted everything as inserted.
- **A nested `db.tenant` inside a transaction sees nothing** (again): the bank statement upload answered 404
  for the row it had just written until the read reused the open client.
- **DATE columns arrive as local-midnight Date objects**; serialised naively they land on the previous day
  in IST. The live rows endpoint hands the screen plain `YYYY-MM-DD` strings.
- **Templates installed by a migration exist only for the schools that existed then**; the fee reminder
  installs `fee_due` on first use so a school created later still sends.
- **The mock router picked `my_children` for "Was my child absent this week?"** because "my child" outscored
  "absent"; the attendance entry now carries the longer phrases.
- **Segregation of duties bit the demo**: the Sprint 5 delegation gave `dev-teacher` the coordinator's role for a
  week, so `exams.marks.enter` conflicted with the delegated `exams.marks.unlock` and her own mark entry was
  refused (correctly). The demo delegation now sits in the past.
- **The seeded hostel demand rows of VI-A roll 4 are ₹0** after a later regeneration, so the Sprint 14 hostel
  receipt stays an advance and the head-wise tally shows it under ADVANCE; a seed fix for Sprint 16.
- **Bank CSV headers are free text** ("Ref No./Cheque No.", "Withdrawal Amt."); exact aliases are tried
  first, then a pattern per column.

Known gaps carried forward: ETL rehearsal 3 and the three-month legacy comparison (no legacy dump here);
registers and analysis views, promotion linkage (Sprint 16); the evaluation set of 200 questions and the
red-team suite (Sprint 16); anomaly alerts are threshold rules (no seasonality); the teacher app has no
Hindi UI (the assistant answers in Hindi and Hinglish regardless).
