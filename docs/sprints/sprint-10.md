## 4. Decisions

- Requests always go through approval; the requester's own permission never bypasses it. This keeps one path and one audit trail for anything that reaches parents at scale.
- Consent is applied at dispatch time, not at composition, so a withdrawal between approval and sending is honoured; service messages are exempt because fees, attendance and safety notices are necessary for the service the family signed up for.
- Delivery receipts are matched by the provider's message id (already stored by the workers) rather than by our id, because providers echo their own ids; the shape is normalised so an adapter per provider maps into it.
- Transport is intentionally minimal (routes and riders). Sprint 12 adds stops, geo, vehicles, slabs and requests on the same tables.
- Queries reuse the class-section scope model of attendance: a class teacher answers their sections; category routing suggests a role but does not lock the query.
