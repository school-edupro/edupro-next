# UAT script for fees (Phase 3, Sprints 12 to 16)

For the pilot school's accounts office and super-users. Each scenario names the login from
`docs/demo-logins.md`, the steps and the expected result. Record pass/fail and remarks in the last two
columns. Sign-off of this script is one of the shadow-run exit criteria (`docs/runbooks/shadow-run.md`).

| --- | -------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------- |
