# Exams and report cards: a guide for the examination cell and teachers

## Who does what

| Person               | Does                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Academic coordinator | Exam types and grade scales, exams and their subjects, entry locks, report-card templates and releases |
| Subject teacher      | Enters marks for their subject and sections (teacher app or admin)                                     |
| Exam office          | Enters marks and codes for every section (admin), e.g. from paper sheets; cannot reopen locked marks   |
| Class teacher        | Indicators, remarks, exam attendance, height and weight (the exam register)                            |
| Principal            | Reads results analytics; approves the release                                                          |
| Families             | Read the released card in the parent app and download the PDF (unless withheld for fee default)        |

## Before the exam

1. **Exams → Exams setup**: one menu entry, four tabs. Each thing is set in one place only; Excel and PDF of each list sit beside it.
   - **Types, grade scales, marks codes**: exam types (PT, half-yearly, annual) and the grade scale per class band. **Edit** on a type changes its name, weightage, order or status; the code stays, and an inactive type is not offered for new exams.
2. **Exams → Exams of the session → New exam**, in three steps:
   - **Details**: the type, name, dates and classes (or start from **a copy of an earlier exam**:
     its classes, subjects, marks, parts and progress card sets come with it). Leave the code blank
     and it is made from the type and the year (HY-2026).
   - **Classes and subjects**: one class at a time. The subjects the class studies are ticked for
     you; type the maximum and pass marks once and **Apply** them to every ticked subject, or **Copy
     from** another class. **One figure** on a row splits that subject into parts (Theory and
     Practical, or Physics, Chemistry and Biology entered by their own teachers). Choose the class's
     indicator or progress card set at the foot. **Save and go to** the next class.
   - **Date sheet**: each paper's date and time (**Apply** one time to every paper) and a note
     printed under it (reporting time, what to bring). **Print** gives one class or every class side
     by side.
     Tick **show on the parent portal** so the date sheet appears in the apps. A subject or a class
     with marks entered can't be taken out of the exam (its marks would be lost).
3. Optional: indicator sets, the remark bank. **Exams → Exams setup → Indicators and HPC** holds the sets in up to
   three levels (area → item → sub-item); **Add the progress card set** brings the NCERT
   foundational-stage Holistic Progress Card for Balvatika to Class II (domains, curricular goals,
   competencies; Beginner / Progressing / Proficient printed with Stream / Mountain / Sky) and **Add
   the progress card template** its card. Choose a class's set on the exam's page. A top item may
   have **own levels** (Always, Mostly, Sometimes, Rarely for observations, learning habits, peer and
   parent sections): its items are rated on those, and the card prints a key line for them.
   **Exams setup → Areas and level scales** holds the lists the set editor picks from: the areas
   (in the order the card prints them and teachers see them; a new name renames the area in every
   set) and the level scales (changing a scale's levels changes them in every set using it; to
   rename a level write its new words in its place, and a level with saved entries stays).
   An item placed under a heading is in the heading's area.

## Following an exam

- The exam list shows each exam's **stage** (Set-up → Marks entry → Verification → Results → Cards
  released), worked out from what is done, how many marks are entered and the **next step** button.
  Filter by type, class or stage.
- An exam opens on its **Overview**: the next step, the figures, and class by class how many marks
  are entered and verified, indicators, remarks, attendance, health and results. Every page of the
  exam has the same tabs: Overview · Set-up · Marks · Indicators · Remarks · Attendance · Results ·
  Reports.
- **Entry locks** on the Overview: one click locks or opens marks, indicators, remarks, attendance or
  health of a class. Locking needs no special right; opening a locked area needs the unlock right.
  **Lock the whole exam** (at the top) asks first.

## Marks entry

- Teachers enter marks in the teacher app **Marks** (or **Exams → open exam → Marks → Enter
  marks**): choose the section and the subject (it opens at once; **Previous / Next** go through every
  section's subjects), type a mark or pick a code; Enter goes to the next student and a mark above the
  maximum shows in red. **Save**, or **Save and submit** once every student who takes the subject has
  a mark or a code. The page asks before unsaved marks are left.
- The coordinator **locks** a subject when entry is complete; a reopen needs a reason and is audited.
- **Who can type marks**: subject and class teachers, and the **Exam office** role (Access → give it to
  an office login). The school admin and the coordinator verify and reopen, so their marks pages
  show the marks as text: entering and reopening are never the same person.
- The exam register belongs to the class teacher: in the admin, **Remarks** (the general remark and
  the categories, saved together), **Attendance → Exam attendance** (the % shows as you type) and
  **Attendance → Health** (height, weight and blood group; the BMI shows as you type).
- The progress card: in the teacher app **Indicators and progress card** (or **Exams → open exam →
  Indicators**) the class teacher moves between the areas (tabs; nothing is lost) and gives each
  child a level per competency, saving every area at once
  (or a written note), then per child writes "All about me", the parent's feedback (from paper) and
  their own observations, and adds portfolio photos (up to twelve; the card prints six). Parents may
  write their feedback in the parent app under **Results**; it is kept as they wrote it. The
  indicators lock of the class closes all of it.

## Results and report cards

- **Results** tab of an exam: **Analysis**, **Mark register** (by section; Excel, and PDF to view or
  download) and **Promotion** (proposals from the results; **Record** sends the ticked decisions to
  People → Promotions, where they are applied). **Work out results** sits on all three.
- **Reports** tab: the analyses (section-wise subjects, ranges, toppers, subject teachers, upload
  status, missing marks), each as Excel and PDF.
- **Date sheet in the apps**: families see their child's papers under **Exam date sheet** in the
  parent app once the exam is shown on the parent portal; teachers see their classes' date sheets in
  the teacher app (**Exam date sheet**).
- Every page of an exam is in English and Hindi (the language switch at the top).

1. **Exams → open exam → Results → Compute**: totals, percentages, grades, ranks per section and class.
2. **Exams → Report cards → Templates**: the layout per band (HPC for primary, CCE for middle,
   components for secondary, theory/practical for senior); preview with a real pupil.
3. **Exams → Report cards → New release**: choose the term, the exams and the template per band; tick
   **hide defaulters** with the minimum balance if the school withholds cards; release.
4. Generate the batch PDF per section from the release; families download their own from the app.
5. **Insights → Results analytics**: pass % and mean by class and section, weakest subjects.
6. **Exams → open exam → Reports**: the section-wise subject analysis, mark ranges (the class's grade
   scale, or your own ranges), toppers (top N in the class or section, at or above / below a %, or a
   subject's), the subject teachers' analysis, the upload status and the missing marks; each with
   Excel and PDF. A locked result scheme's page has **Merit by division**. The report builder has the
   exam marks, exam results and term results datasets for your own reports.
7. **Promotion proposals** (a locked result scheme of the final term): passed → promote to the next
   class, compartment → held for review (the card says "subject to compartment"), failed → retain,
   absent or incomplete → review, the last class → passed out. Choose next year's section, tick and
   send: the decisions go to **People → Promotions** (applied there) and the cards' "Promoted to" is
   filled.

## Remarks by category

**Exams → Exams setup → Remark categories and bank**: name the categories (the old A / B / C / D lists and "any other"),
tie one to a subject (its subject teacher writes it too), to some classes, and choose free text or the
listed remarks only; put remarks in the bank under each category. Teachers write them in the teacher
app **Remarks** (or **Exams → open exam → Remarks**): one per student and category. The remarks lock
of the class closes them; the report card prints them under the remarks.

## Five common questions

1. _A teacher cannot see a section._ They need a teacher assignment for that section and subject this
   year (**Academics → Teacher assignments**).
2. _The card shows the wrong grade._ Check the grade scale assigned to the class for this exam;
   recompute results after fixing it.
3. _A family cannot download the card._ Either the release is not yet released, or the card is
   withheld for a fee balance above the release's minimum.
4. _We need to change a mark after locking._ Reopen the subject (coordinator), correct, lock again;
   recompute results and re-render the card.
5. _Board results._ **Exams → Board results**: upload the CBSE file; pupils match by board roll number
   (kept on the enrolment) or by name; the analysis and the export are available at once.
