# Leave & attendance (staff)

Staff apply for leave; their approvers decide it level by level; balances are credited by policy and
held while a leave waits. Staff attendance is worked out from the biometric punches: late marks and
missing punches turn into leave, and an absent day nobody explains becomes loss of pay. This page
covers the set-up, applying, approving, the balances, staff attendance, and the dashboard and reports.

## Who does what

| Who                                                   | What                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Leave & attendance office** (its own role)          | Keeps the set-up: leave types, policies, weekly offs, shifts, who approves, the settings |
| **School admin**                                      | The same as the leave office                                                             |
| **Approvers** (HOD, reporting manager, named staff …) | Decide the leave and attendance corrections of the staff a rule sends to them            |
| **Every employee**                                    | Applies for leave, sees their attendance and asks for a day to be corrected              |
| **Principal, vice principal, HR**                     | Open the dashboard and every report                                                      |
| **Staff given a report** (an HOD …)                   | Open that report (Leave & attendance reports, in the menu of both apps)                  |

## Where to find it

Every screen is in the side menu's **Employee leave & attendance** (the staff app: **Leave &
attendance**); each shows only to those it is for:

- **Everyone**: My leave, My attendance.
- **When something waits for you, or staff report to you**: Leave to approve, Attendance corrections
  to approve, My team's attendance (the entry goes once nothing waits).
- **The leave office**: Leave register, Leave balances, Attendance register, Late marks and missing

- **Those with the reports**: Leave dashboard, Leave & attendance reports.
- **Who keeps the set-up**: Leave & attendance set-up — leave types, policies, weekly offs, shifts, who

## The set-up (Employee leave & attendance → Leave & attendance set-up)

The page shows six figures, a **To look at** list (what is missing or would misbehave — no policy for
a leave type, staff without an approver, a type ordered after loss of pay …), four sections and the
leave types at a glance. Fill the sections in order.

**1. Leave rules**

- **Leave types** — a school starts with ten: CL, EL, SL, HPL, CO, OD, WFH, ML, PL and LOP. For each:
  paid or **loss of pay** (no pay, no balance, a half day always allowed), **keeps a balance** (credited
  by a policy, checked and held when applied; no for on duty or maternity, which only have their limits),
  counts as present (on duty, work from home), half day allowed, approved at once, document needed, not
  next to other leave, for days ahead, the fewest and most days an application, the most days a month (a
  type without a balance), the **deduction order** (late marks, missing punches and absence take CL, then
  EL … loss of pay last), how often it is credited, carry forward and its cap, for men / women, pay %
  (half-pay leave, for payroll).
- **Leave policies** — how many days a year staff get of a leave type, for all staff or the
  departments, designations, employee types or employees listed. An employee follows the **first policy
  of the type by priority** (lowest number) that takes them in, so give a narrow policy a lower number
  than one for all. Credits come **quarterly, half-yearly or yearly** (never monthly) in the credit
  months of the session — April + half-yearly = April and October; type them to choose others. Days a
  credit (empty: the days a year shared evenly), pro rata on joining, carry forward and cap (the lower
  of the policy's and the type's), the fewest and most days an application, the **sandwich** (a weekly
  off, a declared holiday or an optional holiday between two leave days is charged too), **credit by
  attendance** (the credit shrinks with the days lost in its period: an absent day, loss of pay or a
  salary hold counts 0, a half day half; a day not worked out counts in full), and encashment
  (information for payroll).
- **Who approves** — a rule is for all staff or those picked, and for one leave type or any type. A leave
  goes to the first rule by priority that takes the employee in (a rule for its type before one for any
  type) and to the levels of its length: **up to 7 days, more than 7 up to 30, more than 30**. Each band
  has up to 3 levels: named staff, the reporting manager, the HOD of the employee's department, everyone
  with a designation, or a role. A band with no level leaves it to the next rule. Approvers are worked out
  when the leave is applied, so a new joiner is covered. A level nobody holds is skipped; nobody approves
  their own leave; the same person at two levels in a row approves once. A separate kind of rule sets
  who approves **attendance corrections**. **Test the chain** shows, for an employee, a leave type and so
  many days, the rule and the people at each level today — or why there is nobody.

**2. The calendar**

- **Weekly offs** — weekdays (1 Monday … 7 Sunday) in the weeks of the month you list (2,4 = the 2nd
  and 4th Saturday; empty = every week), or a list of dates (a rotating off), for all staff or those
  picked. A day is off when any rule for the employee makes it off. A school starts with Sunday.
- **Holidays** — kept on the school calendar (holidays for everyone or for employees).

**3. Shifts and timings**

- **Shifts** — start and end (24-hour), grace minutes, the hours for a present day and a half day, the
  shift's hours, overtime, **bus duty** (never marked late), what a day with **one punch only** is (absent,
  half day or present), and the **late / early rule**: late in (after the grace) or out early by at least
  so many minutes makes the day a half day, absent, or half a day of a leave type. A day shift ends after
  it starts; a night shift may end the next morning. A school starts with General (08:00–15:30) and Bus
  duty (06:30–16:30).
- **Shift postings** — which shift an employee works from a day (to a day). A later start wins an
  overlap; a posting of one day changes that day only. Staff without a posting work the default shift.
- **Department and special timings** — hours over the shift: a department's every day, or one
  employee's for some dates (a duty, a late bus), with their own grace, present and half-day hours and
  late / early rule. A special timing wins over the department's, which wins over the shift.

**4. Settings** — late marks for one day of leave (3), missing punches for one day of leave (4), loss of
pay after so many days of absence with no application (7), the date to count late marks from (a fresh
start), the default shift, punches closer than so many minutes are one (10), how far back the nightly
re-check and absent marking look, whether late minutes lower the day's hours, whether the office may
change punch times and apply leave for an employee, how far back leave may be applied, this school's
device serials (for punches read from the central device database), who else gets the missing-punch
e-mails (each address gets a copy), and the message channels (e-mail, WhatsApp, app; SMS once its
templates are approved). Under the settings, **Message templates** shows each leave and correction message
and the channels it can go on: WhatsApp goes only once the name Meta approved is put on its template
(Communication → Templates, search "leave\_").

**5. Reports** — **Who may open the reports** (the principal, the leave office, the school admin and HR
open every one; give a report to other staff here) and **Not required in the reports** (staff left out of
the reports and the dashboard — the management, staff who do not punch; they still apply for leave and
their days are still worked out; picking one of them in a report shows them).

Every grid (types, policies, weekly offs, shifts, postings, Not required) downloads as Excel or PDF and takes an Excel
upload; a wrong value is refused with the reason (a loss-of-pay type marked paid, a quarterly policy
with two credit months, a day shift that ends before it starts).

## Applying (Employee leave & attendance → My leave; staff app → My leave)

The page shows what is left of each leave type this month (days held by leave raised this month already
taken off), the form, your leave and **Waiting for me** (leave you approve). Pick the type and the dates
(one date may be the first or second half of the day), give the reason and — when the type needs one — a
document. The school checks, in this order: half day allowed, the document, the type is for you, days ahead
or how far back, the days counted (weekly offs and holidays are free; between two leave days they count
when the policy's **sandwich** says so), the payroll lock, the fewest and most days, another leave on those
days (the other half of a day is fine), not next to other leave, the balance, the most a month, and that
somebody approves. Two answers come as a question first:

- **The sandwich across two leaves**: a weekly off or holiday between this leave and another of yours is
  counted too — "Apply from … to …?". Yes applies for the longer dates.
- **Not enough balance**: "Apply 3 CL + 2 LOP (loss of pay)?". Yes makes the paid part and a loss-of-pay
  part decided together (a day half paid is paid in its first half).

The days are **held** from your balance as soon as you apply (in the month you apply), and given back if
it is not approved, withdrawn or cancelled. Loss of pay is never applied for on its own.

## Approving

A leave goes through the levels of **Who approves** for its length; each approver sees it under Waiting
for me (admin and staff app) and in My approvals, and gets a message (e-mail, app, WhatsApp — the school's
channels); every morning each approver gets a list of the leave and corrections that wait (3 days or more
in red), worded from its template. Approve, or say why not (needed): the approver gets a confirmation of
what they did and where the leave is now, the next level is told, and so is the employee at the end.
Nobody approves their own leave — their level passes to the next, the last to the school admin; the same
person at two levels approves once. A month locked for payroll cannot be approved into, whichever way the
approval comes. Leave and corrections are decided on their own pages (the general approval steps send you
there). A leave withdrawn by the employee tells the approvers it was withdrawn.

**Leave to approve** (Employee leave & attendance → Leave to approve) has two tabs, **Waiting** (at your
level, the longest waiting first) and **Decided by me** (newest first, one row a leave or its cancellation).
Each shows its figures — waiting, 3 days or more, cancellations asked; or decided, approved, not approved,
cancellations — and a figure clicked lists only those. Filter by search (leave ID, name, code), employee,
department, leave type, leave or cancellation, waiting 3 days or more (Waiting) or what you decided
(Decided by me), and From – To (the leave touching those dates); 25, 50 or 100 a page; **Excel** and **PDF**
of exactly what is filtered, with the letterhead and the Prepared / Checked boxes.

## After applying

- **Withdraw** (yours, still waiting): the days come back at once.
- **Ask to cancel** an approved leave: your approvers decide; the leave stands until they do.
- **The leave office** (Leave register, or the leave's page): **cancel** it now (waiting or approved),
  **decide for the approvers** (with a reason), **shorten** an approved leave from one end, **enter leave for
  an employee** (approved at once; the fewest days and the document are not asked; loss of pay may be
  entered; leave already on those days is replaced — only if the entry is made).

Approved leave fills the daily activity log's leave days; cancelled or cut days are taken out again.

## The leave register and balances

**Leave register** (**Approve / Reject Leave**, as the old screen): nine figure cards for the leave year —
Total Leaves, Pending (at the first level), L1 Approved and L2 Approved (waiting at the next level), Approved
(the final level), Rejected, Cancelled, LWP (loss of pay), Withdrawn — click one to see its leave, again to
see all. **Records per page** (25 / 50 / 100), **Filter** (leave type, employee, department, From – To,
applied by, search; the list opens on this leave year), **Download** (Excel and PDF; the Excel adds each
level: who approved, when, or who it waits with). Each row: Leave ID (with the document and how often its
dates changed), Employee Details (and **On behalf · who** when the office entered it), Leave Type, Applied
Date, From / To Date, Total Days, **Status** (Pending, L1 / L2 Approved, Approved, LWP…), **Final Status**,
Reason (View More), **Last Action** and its date (L1 Approved by whom, Applied & approved on behalf,
Deducted by the system, Rejected by the leave office…), **Current Approver** (LEVEL 2 PENDING and who, or
APPROVED and by whom). The **Action** icons are the office's: while a leave waits, **Approve** or **Reject** it
over every approver (with the reason, kept as the leave office's; the employee is told); once approved,
**Edit dates** (trim the ends; the days cut are given back) and **Cancel**. A rejected, withdrawn or cancelled
leave stays as it is — enter a fresh leave for the employee (**＋ Leave for an employee**) if needed.

**Click a Leave ID** for its **Leave Approval Details** (without leaving the register): the employee, leave
type, status, From — To with the weekdays, days, when and by whom it was applied, the reason; the **Approval
Workflow** level by level (L1, L2, L3 — the approver as code and name, Awaiting or Approved / Rejected, the
remark, the action date and who acted); the **Leave Cancellation Details** the same way; and the **Days**.
**Cancel Leave** (with the reason) is there for a leave the office may cancel; **Full page** opens the
leave's own page (Ctrl / ⌘-click a Leave ID to open it in a new tab). **Leave ID** and **Employee Details**
stay in view when the other columns scroll.

**Employee Leave Balance** (as the old screen): one row per employee × leave type × month, every month newest
first (this month first) — Emp Id, name, leave type, month, year, **Open Bal** (carried from last month),
**Accrued** (credited), **Availed** (the leave raised in the month, waiting or approved), **Correction**,
**Close Bal** and **Lapsed** (once closed), **Total Bal** (Open + Accrued + Correction − Availed) and
**Open / Closed**. The eye opens the row's **Leave Details** (as the old screen): the month it is based on,
the employee, the **policy** (annual entitlement, how it is credited), the **accrual** worked out — the days
in the period the credit counts, the present days, what is credited this month and the sum behind it (in a
month with no credit: the period the next credit counts, so far, and what it comes to if no more days are
lost) — the **attendance** day by day (Is Present, Is LOP, LOP %), and, folded, the balance and every movement
(who, when, why). The pencil **adjusts** this month's balance (only this month is open). **Filter** (employee, department, month,
leave type, search), **＋ Add** (a correction of this month for any employee, with a remark), **Import** (the
opening balances from Excel: the figure you type becomes the balance; running the same sheet again changes
nothing), **Download** (Excel and PDF), and **Run the month now** (closes past months, opens this month and
credits it; the system does it every night).

**How balances move**: credits come in the policy's credit months for the months before (pro rata from the
day of joining); nothing lapses month to month; in the session-end month (the month before the policy's
session start) the balance is kept up to the carry-forward cap, the rest lapses.

## Staff attendance (Employee leave & attendance → Attendance register)

**Punches come in three ways**: the biometric devices send them (each registered as a biometric device
under Attendance → RFID readers), the system reads the school's central device database every 15 minutes
(read only, only the device serials in the settings), and the office loads punches from Excel. A punch is
matched to an employee by their biometric id, else their employee code; punches of an unknown code wait
until someone has it.

**Staff punches** (Employee leave & attendance → Staff punches, as the old Employee / Attendance Details)
shows where the punches come from — each biometric machine (in touch or when last seen, today's punches,
the last punch, how many wait to be worked into their days, unknown IDs in the last 30 days) and the other
sources — with **Process now** for the office. Four tabs:

- **Department** — every raw punch of a department in a date range (up to 31 days): the employee, date,
  day, punch time to the second, the device, the source (machine, central sync, sheet upload, by hand),
  Processed or Waiting, and what the day was marked (it opens the day).
- **Attendance History** — every version of an employee's days (up to 92): the one in force **Active**,
  the ones it replaced **InActive** under it, with what the day was, IN and OUT, who marked it (the system
  for days worked out from the punches) and when, and the remark; the eye shows how the day in force was
  worked out (timing, grace, punches, hours, late, early, result). The record to trust in a dispute.
- **Attendance Log** — one employee's raw punches (up to 92 days), as above.
- **Unmatched** — punches of an ID nobody holds (a new joiner's card, a mistyped ID) and the double taps (a
  second punch within a minute, not counted). **Give to employee** (the office) puts the ID on their record,
  matches its punches to them and works those days out again (not in a month locked for payroll).

Department, Attendance Log and Attendance History download as Excel or PDF.

**A reporting manager** (anyone set as **Reports to** on an employee's Posting) sees **Staff punches** in
the menu for the staff who report to them: **My team** (every punch of all of them, up to 31 days),
**Attendance Log** and **Attendance History** for one of them — not anyone else's, not a department, not the
machines or the unmatched punches. In the staff app the same is under **My team → Punches**. The office
also gets **My team** when staff report to them.

**Who reports to whom** is set in **People → Employees → Reporting managers**: every employee with their
manager this year — change one in its row (Save), tick several and **Assign to the ticked** (or Nobody),
**Move a team** (all of one manager's reportees to another, when a manager leaves or changes role), or
**Excel upload** (the sheet comes filled with today's managers: change the codes, Check shows old → new and
what is refused, then save). One employee can also be changed on their **Posting** tab. A line that would go
round a loop (someone reporting to a person who reports to them) is refused. Leave and corrections already
waiting with the old manager stay with them; new ones go to the new manager.

**How a day is worked out**: the day's shift (a special timing, else the department's, else the shift
posting, else the default shift; a night shift takes the next morning's OUT), first IN and last OUT (two
punches closer than the settings' minutes are one), the hours (less the late minutes when the settings
say so), then present, half day or absent by the hours; one punch only is what the shift says. Late after
the grace makes a **late mark**; the shift's late or early rule can make the day a half day or absent, or
charge half a day of a leave type (once). A worked weekly off or holiday keeps its punches. Leave, a
correction and a day set by hand are never changed by the punches. Every night the system marks weekly
offs and holidays, absent days (no punch, no leave, no correction asked), late marks and missing punches
(an e-mail to the employee), works the last few days out again, and turns absence past the settings' days
into loss of pay.

**The register** (as the old Payroll / Attendance Register): a block per employee — Emp ID, name and
department, then four lines a column a day: **STATUS** (P present, HD half day, A absent, WO, H, the leave's
code with ½ for half a day, LOP loss of pay, HOLD salary hold, · not worked out yet), **IN**, **OUT** and
**HRS**; weekly offs and holidays are shaded. Markers on a day: **L** late mark, **F** a missing (forgotten)
punch, **m** set by hand. On the right, the month's counts — P, A, WO, H, HD, each leave type, HOLD, **L**
late marks, **F** forgotten punches — and **Pay**, the payable days (the month less absent days, half of each
half day, loss of pay and holds; "not marked" says how many past days the system has not worked out).

- **Filter**: month, department, and **Show all staff** — the staff on the "Not required in the reports"
  list (test, vendor or management accounts) are left off the register unless ticked; their days still
  count for payroll.
- **Download**: the register as Excel or as an A3 PDF (four lines an employee), and the **Payroll feed
  (Excel)** — each employee's figures for payroll, with **pay-equivalent days** (a half-pay leave such as
  HPL counted at its pay %) and a second sheet, **Encashment (information)**.
- The strip of tools: **Search** (employee ID or name), **Attendance Status** (only the staff with such a
  day: a code, a leave, L, F or m), **View** (status, IN, OUT and hours, or status only), **Corrections**
  (the corrections staff asked), **Bulk Update**, **Apply Leave** (for an employee, approved at once),
  **Run now** (read the punches, the nightly run, work days out again, deduct late marks), the **Open /
  Locked** button and the **Legend**.
- **Corrections** opens **Attendance Corrections** for the month: every change to a day — the employee, the
  day, **what changed** (Status A → P, IN, OUT), the reason (a leave's number, a correction approved, the
  hand change's reason), **who changed it** and when. ‹ › move a month; search by employee ID, name or who
  made the change; **People only** (ticked) hides what the system worked out itself (from the punches, the
  nightly weekly off / holiday / absent marks). The correction requests staff asked for are linked below it.
- **Click a count** on the right (P, A, a leave, L, F…) for its days: "Absent — 1 day", the dates, and the
  hand corrections made on those days (or "No manual corrections on these days").
- **Click a day** for its quick edit: the status, IN, OUT and the reason; saving sets the day by hand (m).
  **Full day detail** opens the day's page. Making a weekly off or holiday **Absent** asks for a tick: it
  becomes loss of pay once the month is locked.
- **Bulk Update** (**Bulk Attendance Update**, as the old module's dialog), three tabs:
  - **From a spreadsheet**: one row = one employee on one day, P / HD / A / WO / H / HOLD, each day set by
    hand (Manual) with its reason. It never cancels leave: a day under waiting or approved leave is
    **skipped** and named in the preview; leave and LOP go through Apply Leave. **Download Template**, give
    the **Default Reason** (used for every row whose Reason is blank), choose the file (.xlsx or .csv; the
    old module's headings EmpId, DayType, InTime, OutTime are read too), **Preview & Validate (dry run)**,
    then **Save** the good rows. Days to come and locked months are refused.
  - **On screen**: one status (P, A, HD, WO, H) for the employees picked (search, Pick all shown) — or
    everyone in a department — over up to 62 days, with the reason; weekly offs or holidays made Absent
    need the tick.
  - **Punches from a spreadsheet**: the IN and OUT the device could not send; the day is worked out from
    them as from the device.
- **Lock** (a past month): the dialog first lists the missing punches (F) still open and the staff with no
  department; locking turns the absent days nobody explained into loss of pay, then stops every change.
  **Unlock** asks why.

**A day** (click it): how it was worked out in a sentence, the punches with where they came from, the
late mark or missing punch, the leave, the corrections asked and the history (every change, why and by
whom). The office **corrects the day** — IN, OUT, or what the day was — with a reason; a correction
clears the late mark or missing punch it settles. Leave on the day is changed from the leave, not here.

**Late marks and missing punches** (from the register): each month's, by status — counting, charged as
leave, waived, lapsed — with **Waive** / **Clear** (with a reason) for one still counting. At the month's
end every 3 late marks (4 missing punches — the settings) become a day of leave by the deduction order
(CL, then EL … loss of pay last), dated on the first of them; what is left lapses. A day corrected later
takes back what it caused: if its late mark (or missing punch) was already turned into leave, that leave is
cancelled (days and balance back), its other marks count again and a past month is deducted afresh. Each
list downloads as **Excel** or **PDF**.

**Corrections (regularisation)**: an employee opens a day (My attendance) and asks for it to be
corrected — the IN, the OUT or what the day was, with the reason. It goes through the **attendance
corrections** rule of Who approves, like leave; the approver sees it in Waiting for me / My approvals,
opens the day with its punches and approves (the day is set, its late mark or missing punch cleared) or
says why not. The approver is told when a correction is asked and the employee when it is decided. One
correction a day; a locked month takes none.

**The payroll lock**: once a month is over, **Lock** it when payroll is final — the absent days nobody
explained become loss of pay first, then nothing in the month changes (leave, corrections, punches,
corrections by the office). **Unlock** with a reason.

**Run now**: read the new punches; the nightly run; **work days out again** for a range up to yesterday
(after a timing or a shift changes, or for the first month: it also marks weekly offs, holidays, absence
and late marks; missing punches stay with the nightly run's last week); a month's late-mark and
missing-punch deductions.

**From Excel**: **days set by hand** (employee code, date, what the day was or IN / OUT, a remark — the
punches no longer change them) and **punches** the device could not send (employee code, date, time).
Download the sheet, fill it, **Check**, then **Save** the good rows.

**My attendance** (Employee leave & attendance → My attendance; staff app → My attendance): your month — the figures,
each day with IN, OUT, hours, late or early — and the corrections you asked, with where each waits.

**My team's attendance** (Employee leave & attendance → My team's attendance; staff app → My team's attendance): a reporting
manager sees the days of each person who reports to them (the reporting manager on this year's posting)
and opens their days. When leave comes off a past day (cancelled, shortened), the day is worked out again
from its punches, or becomes a weekly off, a holiday or absent — never left blank.

  status shows the leave of the staff who report to you. **On Behalf?** says Yes when the office entered
  the leave, System when the school deducted it (late marks, missing punches, absence).
- **Reporties Attendance**: **Employee**, **From – To** (it opens on the 1st of this month to today). All
  employees: a column a day, up to 31 days; one employee: their days one by one (date, day, attendance,
  in, out, hours, remark), up to 92 days.

## The dashboard and the reports (Employee leave & attendance → Leave dashboard, Leave & attendance reports)

**The dashboard**: twelve figures — staff, in today, on leave today, not in yet (no punch, no leave, a
working day), late today, weekly off or holiday, the month's attendance %, late marks, missing punches and
loss-of-pay days this month, leave and corrections waiting — then today by department, who is on leave
and late today, what waits longest (3 days or more in red), and two small charts: attendance by day this
month and leave taken this year by type. A reminder shows when last month is not locked for payroll.

**The reports** (pick one, filter, read it here — up to 3,000 rows — and save it as **Excel** or **PDF**
with the letterhead and the **Prepared by** (you, with your signature and the time), **Checked by** and
**Principal** boxes):

| Monthly    | Monthly summary                     | Working days, present, half day, absent, weekly off, holiday, paid leave, loss of pay, hold, not marked, late marks, missing punches, attendance % |

The two daily reports show their **figures** over the table (staff, in, not in, on leave, late…). Click a
figure to list only those staff — **Not in** gives the people who have not come; the figure picked is
outlined and the line above says so. Click it again (or **Staff**, or **Show everyone**) for everyone. The
Excel and PDF keep what is picked; a figure of 0 has nobody to show.

**How the figures are counted** (the same in every report): a half day counts half present; on duty and
work from home count present; half a day of leave is half leave and half present; loss of pay is counted
apart from paid leave; a day nothing was worked out for shows **·** ("not marked") and is never counted
absent; **working days** are the days up to yesterday from joining, less weekly offs and holidays;
**attendance %** = (present + paid leave) ÷ working days. A period longer than the report allows (31 days
for early going and basic work, 62 for late arrival and forgot punch, 400 for the annual and leave
reports) is refused with a message, not cut.

## Moving from the old module (Leave & attendance set-up → 6. Moving from the old module)

Once, when the school leaves the old leave module. Nothing in the old database is changed: the workers
read it through a read-only connection IT sets on the server (`LEGACY_LEAVE_DB_URL`).

1. **The months** — the **parallel month** (this month or the next): both systems run, the old one still
   the record; the **freeze date**: approvals stop in the old module; the switch comes on or after it.
2. **Read the old database** — now, and every night of the parallel month. The page shows when and how
   many rows of each table, or why it could not (no connection, a refusal).
3. **Preview** — every step run and undone: what would come, and what could not with examples (an
   employee code not found here, a scope this module does not have, a credit that changes).
4. **Bring the set-up** — leave types (by code), policies (by name; credited by attendance as the old
   module did; monthly credits become quarterly here), weekly offs (the old rules replace the starting
   Sunday rule), staff holidays on the school calendar (a holiday already there on the same dates is kept),
   shifts (by code) and postings, department timings, an employee's timings for some dates (an employee's
   own timing becomes one for the year), who approves (a rule for each old scope, leave type and priority;
   its day ranges fill the bands here; a reporting-manager level stays one), the settings (late marks and
   missing punches a day of leave, time edits, entry for an employee, e-mail, the default shift), the
   reports' "Not required" list and who opens each report. Brought again, the same rows are updated.
5. **Bring the balances** — once the parallel month has begun: the ledger from 1 April to the month before
   the parallel month, closed as the old module closed it; the parallel month opens at that closing and is
   credited by the policies here. The parallel month starts.
6. **Bring this year's history** — leave and its days (an old number kept, else OLD-…), the days before the
   parallel month as the old module had them (the punches here never change them; the day page says they
   came from the old module), late marks and missing punches (those left over then lapsed), corrections,
   and the months the old module locked. **Bring it again each day of the parallel month**: new and
   changed leave comes, approved leave goes onto the days here and what waits is held from the balance —
   nothing is doubled.
7. **Compare and switch** — the comparison lists every employee-day whose code differs between the two
   systems and every balance that differs (with this month's credit of each: the old module credits
   monthly, this one quarterly). After the freeze date, read the old database once more and **Switch**: the
   last leave and corrections come, those still waiting start at the first level of Who approves here
   (approvers see them in Waiting for me / My approvals), and the old module is no longer read. From then
   on staff apply in EduPro only.

## Common questions

1. **A teacher gets no CL.** Leave types at a glance shows how many staff are in no policy of the type:
   add a policy for them, or widen one.
2. **Who would approve a 10-day leave of this teacher?** Who approves → Test the chain.
3. **The Accounts staff should go to the accountant first.** Add a rule for the Accounts department with
   a lower priority number than the rule for all staff, and set its levels.
4. **Second and fourth Saturdays are off.** Weekly offs: weekdays 6, weeks 2,4.
5. **A new joiner has no approver.** Rules are worked out when the leave is applied: if the joiner's
   department, designation or type is in a rule, they are covered at once.
6. **"Not enough CL" though the teacher had days last month.** Balances are credited in the credit months
   only; check Balances (the trail says what came and went) or load the opening balances.
7. **The HOD applied for leave: who approves it?** Their own level passes on; the last level goes to the
   school admin.
8. **The register is all dots.** Nothing is worked out yet: check the device serials in the settings and
   that the workers run, or **Run now → work days out again** for the dates.
9. **A teacher's punch is not on the register.** Their biometric id (or employee code) must match the
   device's; the punch waits until it does. Look in **Staff punches → Unmatched**: give the ID there to the
   teacher and their punches are matched and the days worked out.
10. **The exam duty came late by bus: no late mark, please.** Add a special timing for those dates, then
    work the days out again — or waive the late marks.
11. **Payroll wants the days.** Lock the month, then **Payroll feed (Excel)**.
12. **The correction button is missing.** The day is today or later, the month is locked, or a correction
    for the day already waits or was approved.
13. **The HOD should see the daily report of the staff.** Set-up → Who may open the reports → Daily
    attendance → add the HOD. They find it under Employee leave & attendance → Leave & attendance
    reports (staff app: Leave & attendance → Leave & attendance reports).
14. **The director should not be in the reports.** Set-up → Not required in the reports → add them.
15. **The monthly summary shows "not marked" days.** The nightly run has not worked those days out (the
    workers stopped, or the first month): Attendance register → Run now → work days out again.
16. **The preview says an employee was not found.** The old employee id must be the employee code here (or
    their biometric id); correct the employee's code here, read again and preview again.
17. **A balance differs in the comparison.** First the credit: the old module credited every month and
    this one credits quarterly, so a month without a credit differs by the old month's accrual. Anything
    else: open the balance's trail (Balances) and correct it with a reason.
