# Certificates

One register for every certificate the school issues to its students: the transfer certificate (TC),
bonafide, character and date of birth so far (more types, staff certificates, requests from the apps
and a QR check follow). Each certificate gets the next number of its type and year, which is never
given again, even when it is cancelled. Every field it prints is kept as it was on the day, so a
reprint or a duplicate is the same paper whatever changes later.

## Who does what

| Who                                   | What                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Certificate office** (its own role) | Issues certificates after their checks, prints duplicates, cancels, keeps the register |
| **Principal** (the override right)    | Issues a certificate whose check is not clear, with a reason (recorded)                |
| **School admin**                      | All of the above                                                                       |
| **Academic coordinator, auditor**     | See the register and open any certificate                                              |

## Where to find it

Side menu → **Certificates → Certificate register**. **Issue a certificate** opens from the register's
header and from a student's profile (**Status** tab → Certificates card, or **Issue the TC**).

## Issuing a certificate

1. **Type and student.** Choose the certificate, then find the student by name or admission number;
   past students are found too (marked **Left**).
2. **Checks.** Each type checks what it needs before it can be issued:

   | Certificate   | Checks                                                                                                                                                     |
   | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | TC            | The withdrawal is complete (or the student has left); no fees due; library books and dues cleared; no hostel bed; school transport ended; one TC a student |
   | Bonafide      | A student on the rolls                                                                                                                                     |
   | Character     | Any student, on the rolls or past                                                                                                                          |
   | Date of birth | Any student, on the rolls or past                                                                                                                          |

   A check that is not clear names what is wrong (e.g. "₹4,500.00 due up to 31-03-2027"). From a
   withdrawal, a department that has cleared (or was bypassed) there answers its check.

3. **What it prints.** The fields are filled from the record where it holds them (the TC's working
   days and days present, subjects, the month the dues are paid to, any concession …) and can be
   changed before issue. **Update the preview** shows the paper as it will print; the number shows as
   "(next)" until it is issued.
4. **Issue.** The certificate takes its number (e.g. `TC/2026-27/00005`; the TC also takes its book
   and Sl. no., 100 to a book), its PDF is made, and its page opens. A TC also takes the student off
   the rolls, unless the withdrawal is still running (then the withdrawal's completion does).

**When a check is not clear**, only the principal can issue it anyway: the screen asks why, and the
reason is printed nowhere but kept on the certificate and shown in the register (**Checks → Issued past
a failed check**). It needs a recent two-step sign-in. "Already holds this certificate" is never
overridden: open the one held and print a duplicate, or cancel it first.

## Duplicates and cancelling

On a certificate's page, **Duplicate or cancel**:

- **Print a duplicate** (with a reason, e.g. the original was lost): the same number and paper,
  printed **DUPLICATE (copy 1)**, then copy 2 …
- **Cancel** (with a reason): the certificate and its duplicates show as cancelled; a TC's own record is
  cancelled too. The number is not given again. Cancelling a TC does not put the student back on the
  rolls: revoke the withdrawal for that.

## The register

Six figures (all, issued, this month, duplicates, cancelled, issued past a check), then filters: type,
status, originals or duplicates, issued past a check, year, issue dates, and a number, student or
admission number. **Download** gives the list as filtered, as Excel or PDF. Each row opens its
certificate; the eye opens the PDF, the arrow saves it.

## The five most common questions

1. _The TC says "No withdrawal is complete"._ Finish the student's withdrawal first (People →
   Withdrawals), or issue the TC from the withdrawal itself; otherwise the principal issues it with a
   reason.
2. _The working days show 0._ The class's day attendance was not marked that year; type the figures
   from the attendance register before issuing.
3. _We changed the TC template; will old TCs change?_ No. Every certificate keeps the template version
   it was made with; new ones use the new version.
4. _A parent lost the TC._ Open it in the register and print a duplicate: same number, marked
   DUPLICATE.
5. _Where are the TCs issued before the register?_ In the register under their old numbers (they print
   from the TC template of the time), and in People → Transfer certificates.
