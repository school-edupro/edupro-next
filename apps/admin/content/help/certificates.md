# Certificates

One register for every certificate the school issues: for students the transfer certificate (TC),
bonafide, character, date of birth and its justification, study, caste, residence, no objection, travel,
visa, scholarship, board registration and fee paid; for staff experience, service and visa — and any type
the school adds (requests from the apps, the QR check and bulk issue follow). Each certificate gets the next number of its type and year, which is never
given again, even when it is cancelled. Every field it prints is kept as it was on the day, so a
reprint or a duplicate is the same paper whatever changes later.

## Who does what

| Who                                   | What                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Certificate office** (its own role) | Issues certificates after their checks, prints duplicates, cancels, keeps the register |
| **Principal** (the override right)    | Issues a certificate whose check is not clear, with a reason (recorded)                |
| **School admin**                      | All of the above                                                                       |
| **Academic coordinator, auditor**     | See the register and open any certificate                                              |

## Where to find it

Side menu → **Certificates → Certificate register**. **Issue a certificate** opens from the register's
header and from a student's profile (**Status** tab → Certificates card, or **Issue the TC**). **Certificates
→ Certificate set-up** holds who signs, the types and the templates.

## Issuing a certificate

1. **Certificate and person.** Choose the certificate; the next box follows it — **Student** for a student
   certificate, **Employee** for a staff one. Click it for the list (students on the rolls in class, section
   and roll order; employees in service), or type to narrow it: a name, admission no. or class (e.g. VI-A);
   for staff a name, employee code, designation or department. Those the certificate wants come first —
   for a TC or an experience certificate, those who have left (marked **Left**). Choose one (or use the
   arrow keys and Enter) to see the checks and the paper. A type with more than one layout (bonafide:
   with both parents or the father only) asks which.
2. **Checks.** Each type checks what it needs before it can be issued:

   | Certificate   | Checks                                                                                                                                                     |
   | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | TC            | The withdrawal is complete (or the student has left); no fees due; library books and dues cleared; no hostel bed; school transport ended; one TC a student |
   | Bonafide      | A student on the rolls                                                                                                                                     |
   | Character     | Any student, on the rolls or past                                                                                                                          |
   | Date of birth | Any student, on the rolls or past                                                                                                                          |

   A check that is not clear names what is wrong (e.g. "₹4,500.00 due up to 31-03-2027"). From a
   withdrawal, a department that has cleared (or was bypassed) there answers its check.

3. **What it prints.** Two groups, every box filled and changeable before issue:
   - **From the student's record** (or the employee's): the person's details the chosen layout prints,
     starting with the name — for a TC its points 1 to 8 (name, father's and mother's name, nationality,
     SC/ST/OBC, the date and class of first admission, the date of birth in figures and words, the class
     last studied), then ID numbers such as PEN and APAAR. A change prints on **this certificate only**:
     the student record stays as it is (correct the record in People for the next ones). A changed box
     turns amber and shows what the record says; the date of birth in words follows the date unless typed.
     The guardian's name is asked only when there is no father's name; the admission number always
     prints from the record. The certificate's page lists each change under **Changed from the record**,
     and a re-issue or correction carries them.
   - **The certificate's own details**, filled from the record where it holds them (the TC's working
     days and days present, subjects, the month the dues are paid to, any concession …).

   **Update the preview** shows the paper as it will print; the number shows as "(next)" until it is
   issued.

4. **Issue.** The certificate takes its number (e.g. `TC/2026-27/00005`; the TC also takes its book
   and Sl. no., 100 to a book), its PDF is made, and its page opens. A TC also takes the student off
   the rolls, unless the withdrawal is still running (then the withdrawal's completion does).

**When a check is not clear**, the page shows the checks and what to do next, and keeps **What it
prints** and the preview back until they are clear — for a TC, a link to the student's withdrawal (or to
start one on the student's page, Status): its departments clear the fees, library, hostel and transport.
Whoever may issue past a check (the principal; the school admin) gets **Issue it anyway, with a reason**,
which opens them; **Keep it back until the checks are clear** closes them again. Issuing it anyway: the screen asks why, and the
reason is printed nowhere but kept on the certificate and shown in the register (**Checks → Issued past
a failed check**). It needs a recent two-step sign-in. "Already holds this certificate" is never
overridden: open the one held and print a duplicate, or cancel it first.

## Duplicates, re-issue and cancelling

A certificate keeps what it printed on the day it was issued: the signatory, the layout, the school's
and the person's details. A later change in the set-up does not alter it. On a certificate's page,
**Duplicate, re-issue or cancel**:

- **Print a duplicate** (with a reason, e.g. the original was lost): the same number and paper,
  printed **DUPLICATE (copy 1)**, then copy 2 …
- **Re-issue with changes**: the Issue page opens filled in with this certificate — the certificate and the
  person stay fixed; correct the purpose, the details typed at issue, the date (today when left empty) or
  the layout, **Update the preview** to see the paper, say why, then **Re-issue with these changes**. A new
  number; the earlier one is cancelled in the same step. If the person holds only one of this kind, the one
  being replaced does not count.
- **Re-issue with today's settings** (with a reason, e.g. issued before the signatory was set): a new
  certificate with a new number for the same person, purpose, layout and details, printed with the
  signatory, layout and school details as they are now, after the type's checks. The earlier one (and its
  duplicates) is cancelled in the same step; each points to the other. If a check fails now, nothing
  changes; one issued past a failed check carries the principal's reason over, which needs the
  principal's right and a fresh sign-in.
- **Cancel** (with a reason): the certificate and its duplicates show as cancelled; a TC's own record is
  cancelled too. The number is not given again. Cancelling a TC does not put the student back on the
  rolls: revoke the withdrawal for that.

A cancelled certificate that was not re-issued has **Issue again with today's settings** and **Issue
again with changes**.

**Keeping the number on a correction.** If your school wants a corrected certificate to keep its number, tick
**Corrections keep the certificate number** in Certificate set-up → Signatory and print. A certificate then has
**Correct this certificate** instead of the re-issue buttons: the Issue page opens filled in (the certificate and
person fixed); correct the purpose, the typed details, the date or the layout, say why, and **Save as revision 2**
(3, 4 …). The person's details, the signatory and the layout print as they are now. The paper prints **Revision n
· corrected on …** at the foot, the public check says it was corrected (the number with the date first printed
still checks), and the certificate's **Changes** list keeps every revision — who, when, why and what changed
(old → new) — each with its own PDF. Duplicates printed after a correction carry it. The register marks them
**Rev n**. In the
register, **Re-issue** and **Re-issued** mark the two.

## The register

Six figures (all, issued, this month, duplicates, cancelled, issued past a check), then filters: type,
status, originals or duplicates, issued past a check, year, issue dates, and a number, student or
admission number. **Download** gives the list as filtered, as Excel or PDF. Each row opens its
certificate; the eye opens the PDF, the arrow saves it. A certificate's PDF is kept for good. One that was
cleared before 16 Nov 2026 (other files are kept seven days) is made again from the same paper when it is
opened: a short "Opening the PDF" step, then the PDF opens by itself.

## The five most common questions

1. _The TC says "No withdrawal is complete"._ Finish the student's withdrawal first (People →
   Withdrawals), or issue the TC from the withdrawal itself; otherwise the principal issues it with a
   reason.
2. _The working days show 0._ The class's day attendance was not marked that year; type the figures
   from the attendance register before issuing.
3. _We changed the TC template; will old TCs change?_ No. Every certificate keeps the template version
   it was made with; new ones use the new version.
4. _A parent lost the TC._ Open it in the register and print a duplicate: same number, marked
   DUPLICATE.
5. _Where are the TCs issued before the register?_ In the register under their old numbers (they print
   from the TC template of the time), and in People → Transfer certificates.

## Requests from the apps

Parents ask for a certificate for a child in the **parent app** (Certificates → Ask for a certificate),
students for themselves, and staff for an experience, service or visa certificate in the **staff app**
(Office → My certificates): the type, the purpose, the copies and a note. What each may ask for is set
on the type (Set-up → the type → **Requests online**).

1. **Approval.** The request goes through the type's levels in order — the certificate office, the
   student's class teacher, the principal (the signatory in the settings) or a role. Each level finds it
   in **Approvals** (the inbox) and in **Certificates → Certificate register → Requests**, where the
   **Waiting with me** filter and figure show yours. Approve, or reject with a reason the requester sees.
   The everyday certificates are approved by the office in one step; the TC and the character
   certificate by the office, then the principal.
2. **Fee.** When the type charges a fee, an approved request puts it on the student as a misc fee
   (copies × the fee); the parent pays it online or at the counter. The office can **take the fee off**
   with a reason.
3. **Issue.** A request that is approved and paid shows **Ready to issue**; **Issue it** opens the issue
   screen for that student and type. The checks run as for any certificate. Issuing marks the request
   issued.
4. **Download.** The requester is told in the app at each step and downloads the PDF from the same page.
   A request not yet issued can be withdrawn by the requester.

**What the parent sees.** The parent app's **Certificates** page, for the child chosen at the top:

- three figures — certificates **ready**, requests **in progress**, requests waiting **to pay**;
- **Your certificates** — every certificate of the child once, newest first, with its number, date and
  purpose; awards and achievements are marked **Award**. The eye opens the PDF and the arrow saves it,
  straight away (the PDF the office made is the one opened; if none is kept, it is made and opens by itself
  in a few seconds). A parent is only ever given that child's own page, never a class batch's file;
- **Requests** — each open request with its steps (Asked → Approved → Fee → Being made → Ready, the Fee
  step only when there is one) and what to do next: **Pay the fee** or **Withdraw**; closed ones fold
  under **Earlier requests**;
- **Ask for a certificate** — the certificates open to the parent, each with its fee (**Free** or the
  amount a copy) and who decides; tap one and its short form opens under it. With more than five, a search
  box narrows the list.

The **Requests** page shows six figures (waiting for approval, waiting with me, waiting for the fee,
ready to issue, issued this month, the oldest open request in days), filters (status, type, waiting with
me, a name or number) and each request's stage, who it waits with and its age.

## Checking a certificate (QR)

Every certificate prints a **QR code** at its foot with its own link. Anyone holding the paper — another
school, an embassy, an employer — scans it (or opens the school's check page,
`<public site>/<school code>/verify`, and types the number and the date of issue) and sees, without
signing in: **Valid** or **Cancelled**, the type, number, date of issue, the school, and the holder's name
in part (e.g. "A\*\*\*v S\*\*\*\*a") to compare with the paper. A duplicate has its own QR and shows as
"duplicate 1" of the same number. The page allows a few checks a minute from one place.

Each check is recorded: a certificate's page (**Public checks**) shows when it was checked, by QR or by
number, and what it answered — never who checked. Certificates issued before the QR get one the next time
their PDF is opened.

## Bulk issue, awards, the TC book and reports

From the register's header:

- **Bulk issue** — choose the certificate, then who:
  - a **student certificate**: **Classes and sections** in one tick list (tick a class for all its sections,
    or single sections; **All classes**; a search; the students on the rolls beside each), or **the
    leavers** (a withdrawal cleared or complete and no TC yet);
  - a **staff certificate**: **Departments** in one tick list (**Employees in service** or **Those who have
    left**; **All departments**; the employees beside each).

  **Preview** lists everyone in class, section and roll order (staff: department and Emp. ID) with their
  checks and a tick each: those clear are ticked; untick anyone who should not get one. One who already
  holds it cannot be ticked; one whose checks are not clear only by the principal, with a reason.
  **Issue to … ticked** gives each its own number and makes a batch and **one PDF for each section or
  department** (at most 600 at a time). The next page lists the batches, each with View and Download as
  its PDF is made, and **Download all (ZIP)** once every PDF is ready; each batch's page lists who got one
  and who was left out, with the reason.

- **From Excel** (Bulk issue → **From Excel — each with their own details**) — when each person needs their
  own details, e.g. the Class XII TCs after the board result:
  1. **Download the sheet**: choose the certificate and who (classes and sections, the leavers, or
     departments). The sheet has a row for each person, **filled from the records**, with the columns that
     certificate needs: the admission no. (employee code for staff), name and class to help, the date of
     issue, the purpose (not for a TC), the layout (a type with more than one), the certificate's own
     details (for a TC: reason, application and struck-off dates, exam result, conduct …, list answers as
     drop-downs) and what it prints about the person (name, parents, date of birth …). A **How to fill**
     sheet explains it.
  2. **Fill or correct it** in Excel. A cell left empty prints as the record has it; a changed person's
     detail prints on that certificate only (the record stays as it is). Delete the rows of those who
     should not get one.
  3. **Upload and check**: nothing is issued yet. The upload's page shows the figures (rows, ready, need
     attention, issued, withdrawal not started) and each row as **Ready** or with **what to fix** — the
     admission no. not found, a second row of the same person, a missing or wrong detail, a check not clear.
  4. **Fix and upload again**: **Download what to fix** gives those rows with the reason in the last column;
     correct them (or the record, the fees, the withdrawal) and **Upload a corrected sheet** into the same
     upload — its rows replace the same people's, the rest stay. **Check again** re-checks every row (after
     the departments have cleared, for instance). **See the paper** opens a row on the Issue page.
  5. **TCs without a withdrawal**: **Start the withdrawals for …** (needs the withdrawal right) starts them in
     bulk with one leaving date and reason; the departments clear them as usual; then **Check again**.
  6. **Issue the … ready**: each gets its number in order and its own details; a PDF for each section or
     department (the run's page has them and the ZIP). The rows needing attention stay to fix and issue
     later; **Earlier uploads** keeps every upload.

- **Awards** — the awards the school gives (set-up: each printed on the achievement, merit, participation
  or award certificate), and who received them: add one (admission no., event, place, date) or upload a
  CSV (admission_no, award, event, position, date, remarks). The file is checked first and nothing is
  saved while a line is wrong; tick **Save them** to save a clean file. Tick the awards and **Issue the
  ticked certificates**: a batch a certificate type, each with its award, event, place and date.
- **TC book** — every TC by book and Sl. No. (100 to a book), with filters and Excel / PDF.
- **Reports** — for a period: by type, by month, by who issued them, those issued past a check, the
  requests (asked, issued, not approved, open, days to issue) and the public checks; each as Excel / PDF.

The achievement certificates of Parent engagement → Achievement certificates are numbered by the register
now (`ACH/2026-27/00001`) and appear in it; their PDF stays the batch's.

## Set-up (Certificates → Certificate set-up)

The page shows six figures, a **To look at** list (no signatory yet, a signatory with no signature image,
a type with no template) and four sections:

1. **Signatory and print** — find the principal (or whoever signs) among the employees; their name, Emp. ID,
   designation and the **signature image on their employee record** print in the last box (untick it to
   sign by hand). The **Prepared by** box prints who issued the certificate with the date and time; the
   **Checked by** box is left to sign. A line at the foot (e.g. "Not valid without the school seal").
2. **Certificate types** — the list shows six figures, a search and **For** / **Status** filters, then the
   student and the staff certificates in two tables. Each row gives the next number, what is checked before
   issue and who may ask for it online, in words, with an **Edit** button. **Add a type** (top right) asks
   for a name, students or staff, a number prefix and the layout to start from; its page opens next.
   A type's page shows its figures (issued, this year, requests waiting, the next number, layouts, on or
   off). **Basics** stays open; each other part is folded with a line saying what is set — open one to
   change it, and **Save changes** (at the bottom of the screen) keeps every part at once:
   - **Layout** — the layout it prints with (**See and edit the layout**), and other layouts the office
     may choose at issue (untick one to stop offering it; **Add another layout**).
   - **Checks before issue** — which students (or employees) can get it, how many one person may hold,
     what must be cleared first, and all classes or only some (tick them).
   - **Who may issue it** — everyone who issues certificates, or only some of those roles.
   - **Asked for online** — who may ask (parents, students, staff), the copies, a fee a copy, the purposes
     to choose from and who decides, in order (the arrows move a level).
   - **Details asked at issue** — the school's own details for the office to fill (each a label, a kind —
     text, date, number or a list — and required or not).
   - **More** — a note for the office, the place in lists and the fixed code.
3. **Templates** — the layouts certificates print with. The list shows six figures, a search, and two
   tables:
   - **Used by a certificate type**: a type prints with it — issuing that certificate uses this paper.
     Changing it changes certificates issued from then on; those already issued keep their paper.
   - **Used by no type**: issuing never picks it — the Issue page lists certificate types, and a
     layout shows there only when a type uses it. To use one, press **Use for a type** on its row:
     choose the type (the one in the layout's name is chosen for you) and how — **Offer it as a choice
     at issue** (the type keeps its layout; on the Issue page a **Layout** list offers this one too,
     after choosing the person) or **Print every certificate of the type with it** (from now on;
     certificates already issued keep their paper). Give a layout to the kind of certificate it was
     made for: it prints that type's details. The same can be done on the type's page, under
     **Layout**. Some here are still in use and say so — keep them:
     **Old-system reprints** (Certificate from the old system (reprint): it reprints certificates
     brought from the earlier system; no type may use it), **TCs from before the register** (the TCs
     issued before the register print with it) and **Achievement certificates screen** (Parent
     engagement → Achievement certificates picks its layout itself; the count says how many it printed).

   Each row says where the layout comes from (EduPro's ready layout, changed by the
   school, or the school's copy), its paper and when it changed, with three buttons: **See** (the paper
   with a sample from the school, in a pop-up), **Edit** and **Copy**. **Make a copy** (top right) does
   the same for any layout; give the copy to a type on the type's page, under Layout.
   A layout's page shows which types use it, **Make a copy** and **Back to the ready layout** (EduPro's
   layout again; the school's changes stay under Versions). **How it prints** shows the paper with a
   sample of the type you choose, and changes a moment after you type. Beside it: the name, the paper and
   the **Wording**. Words in `{{ }}` are filled in for each certificate: put the cursor where one should
   go, then **Insert a detail** (find it by its plain name, e.g. father, class, date). The buttons add
   EduPro's blocks (**Letterhead**, **Signature boxes**, **DUPLICATE mark**, **Footer: number and QR**),
   **B** (bold), **New paragraph** and **Line break**. Sizes and fonts are under **Advanced: styles**.
   **Save changes** (at the bottom of the screen) makes a new version for the next certificates; those
   already issued keep theirs. **Versions** lets you use an earlier one again. A layout may hold text,
   tables, styles and images as data, never scripts or addresses on the internet.

4. **From the old system** — bringing the earlier system's certificates across, once (below).

## From the old system (Certificate set-up → From the old system)

For the move from the earlier system. Nothing in the old system is changed, and nothing is typed here to reach
it: IT sets the server's read-only connection.

1. **Read the old register** — the server reads the old system's certificates and the TC book's last Sl. No.
   The page shows when it was last read. Read again just before you bring them, so the newest come too.
2. **Each old certificate name and its type** — every name the old system used, with how many it has, how many
   it deleted, and for how many EduPro found the student (by admission no.) or the employee (by employee code).
   EduPro suggests a type (TC, bonafide, character, experience …): change it, or choose **Not brought**, then
   **Save the types**.
3. **Check first (nothing saved)** says how many would come and why any are left out. **Bring them** brings each
   one once, under its old number and date (a TC with its book and Sl. No.); one the old system deleted comes
   cancelled. Bringing again later adds only the new ones: nothing comes twice.
4. **The numbers that carry on** — for each type and year, the old system's last number and the next one here;
   the TC book goes on after the old Sl. No. No number is given twice.
5. **The count check** — read = brought + left out (no type chosen, or no student or employee found). For a
   left-out one, add the student or choose its type, then bring again.

A brought certificate sits in the register marked **Old system**. It can't be changed, but it can be printed
again (with a line saying it comes from the old system), given a duplicate or cancelled. Its QR check says the
same.
